Campanula

Common Name: Bellflower, Carpathian Bellflower

Overview

Plant Type      Perennial
Hardiness Zone3

Cultivars

CultivarHeightSpreadBloomsSunPlanted
Carpathian Bellflower 'Rapido Blue'5-7"6-8"Late spring to mid-summerFull SunHouse garden beds

Ongoing Management

Fertilization

Add balanced fertilizer in Spring.

Pruning / Cutting Back

Does not require pruning.

No special care for over-wintering. Mulch with organic matter if hard freeze is expected.

Propagation

Propagate by seed or basal cuttings.

Plant can be invasive; it should be divided every 3-5 years to control spreading. Divide in Spring or Fall.
