Caryopteris Beyond Midnight

  • Major pollinator which provides late season food for bees & butterflies.
  • Not considered invasive

Ongoing Management

Watering

Extended temps above 90 °F requires watering.

Pruning / Cutting Back

Flowers on new shoots. Deadhead spent flowers.

Dies back in winter (zones 5 & 6). Mulch to protect over wintering.

Prune in Spring down to where new growth is sprouting.

Propagation

Beyond Midnight is not permitted to be propagated.
