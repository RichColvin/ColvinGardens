Ongoing Management

Planting

Top of plant should be just below soil level and 36" (3') apart.

Fertilization

 

Once per year

Watering

 

Average water needs. Water regularly, do not overwater

Pruning / Cutting Back

 

None, the dead flowerheads can be left on the plants to provide additional interest in winter
