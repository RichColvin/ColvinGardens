Ongoing Management

Fertilization

Mulch with dry mulch, such as straw, to keep dry in Winter.

No feeding required.

Watering

Water sparingly during the growing season; let it dry between waterings.

Let Ice Plant dry out before Winter.

Pruning / Cutting Back

Deadheading does not typically have an effect to promote more blooming.
