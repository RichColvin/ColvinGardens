Dicentra spectabilis

Common Name: Bleeding Heart

CAUTION

Bleeding Heart plants are not only toxic to humans, but animals as well.

Overview

Plant TypePerennial
Hardiness Zone3

Valentine
Dicentra spectabilis		2-3'2-3'Late Spring to early SummerPartial Sun(none yet)

This plant contains soquinoline alkaloids. Alkaloids negatively affect animals, most commonly cattle, sheep, and dogs. If ingested, all parts may cause stomach upset.

The foilage may aggravate skin allergies. Wear gloves and other protective equipment when handling.
