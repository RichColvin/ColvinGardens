Helenium

Common Name: Sneezeweed

Overview

Plant Type      Perennial
Hardiness Zone 

Cultivars

CultivarHeightSpreadBloomsSunPlanted
Autumnale 'Salsa'18-24"18-24"Mid seasonFull SunHouse garden beds

Attracks butterflies.

Taller cultivars need staking

For best effect, plant in groups of 3 or 5.

Ongoing Management

Fertilization

One application of balanced flower fertilizer in Spring.

Watering

Not drought tolerant; water in absence of water.

Mulch to conserve moisture.

Pruning / Cutting Back

Deadhead spent flowers to encourage continuous blooming. Cut flower stalks down to foliage.

Pinching in Spring to encourage shorter, stronger, sturdy plants.

Overwintering: cover with 6" of hay over mulch. Remove this in the Spring.
