Short-lived perennials. Must be divided regularly or they die out in a few years.

Ongoing Management

Fertilization

Fertilize in Spring with ½" of compost. Do not overfeed as it decreases blooms.

Watering

Likes consistently moist soil.

Pruning / Cutting Back

Prune flowers after spent to encourage leaf production and help ensure repeated blooming all Summer and into Fall.

Spring: Trim back battered foliage.

Late Fall: Remove yellow or dead leaves & spent stems.

Winter: Mulch to prevent the crown from heaving.

Site Selection & Preparation

Thrives in part shade, but will grow in full shade. Best site is a semi-shady location.

Plant in humus-rich, well-drained soil.
