Thrives in shade or part shade.

Maturity:

  • Smaller varieties mature in 3-5 years
  • Larger varieties mature in 5-7 years

Needs 6 weeks of temps below 42° to enter dormancy.

Ongoing Management

Fertilization

Fertilize by adding a healthy layer of compost to soil in Spring.

Pruning / Cutting Back

Deadheading: clip flower stalks after flowers fade.

Late Fall - Cut plants back to ground. Clean away the dead foliage after frost to prevent insect problems. Cover with mulch to over-winter.
