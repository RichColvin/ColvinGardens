Kniphofia

Common Name: Red Hot Poker

Overview

Plant Type      Perennial
Hardiness Zone5

Cultivars

CultivarHeightSpreadBloomsSunPlanted

Gold Rush 36-48" 30-36" Summer Full Sun House garden beds
Orange Blaze 24-30" 18-24" Early to Mid-Summer Full Sun Bee garden

Attracts hummingbirds, bees, & butterflies.

Ongoing Management

Fertilization

Typically do not need to be fed if in nutritious soil.

Pruning / Cutting Back

Deadheading: When towers begin to fade, cut away stem about ½" below flowers.

Spring: Trim foliage a few inches above the ground before new growth emerges.

Fall: Remove all foliage down to the base of the plant. Winterize by covering the crowns with mulch.

Divide plants every few years to extend the lifespan.
