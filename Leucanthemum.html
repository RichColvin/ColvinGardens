Ongoing Management

Fertilization

Spring - Fertilize yearly with compost or other organic materials.

Watering

Requires water during droughts & hot temps.

Pruning / Cutting Back

Deadheading - Remove spent blooms throughout the growing season.

Once foliage begins to fade, prune the foliage to near ground level. Cover with an extra layer of mulch to protect for Winter.
