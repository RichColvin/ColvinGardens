Ongoing Management

Fertilization

Spring - Feed with slow-release shrub/tree fertilizer or organic plant food.

Watering

Needs 1" of water/week until established; once established, is drought tolerant.

Pruning / Cutting Back

Will die back in colder climates.

Fall - Trim off dead, browned leaves.

Late Winter / Early Spring - Shear foliage back to the ground before growth begins. Cover with leaves during intense cold snaps.

Propagation

Unnecessary to divide, but can do so every 3-4 years.
