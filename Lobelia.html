Lobelia

Common Name: Cardinal Flower

CAUTION

Contains toxic alkaloid compounds.

Overview

Plant TypePerennial

individual plants are short-lived, but perpetuates itself by constantly self-seeding & by sending out off-shoots.

Ongoing Management

Fertilization

Late Winter / Early Spring - Add compost.

Watering

Mulch around base of plant to retain moisture.

Pruning / Cutting Back

Can remove dead heads to encourage further blooming, however that may not allow plants to self-seed (which impacts the next year's plants).

Divide plants every 2-3 years.
