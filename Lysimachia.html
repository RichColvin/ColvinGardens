Lysimachia lanceolata

Common Name: Lance-leaved Loosestrife

Overview

Plant TypePerennial

Common for this plant to live 10 years or more. Attracts bees & butterflies.

Most types spread aggressively; regular thinning is needed to keep the plants under control. Can also install a bamboo barrier.

Ongoing Management

Fertilization

Spring - Add a balanced, slow-release fertilizer.

Pruning / Cutting Back

Winter - Goes dormant during the winter; at that point, cut back the plant close to the ground.
