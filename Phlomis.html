Phlomis russeliana

Common Name: Jerusalem Sage, Turkish Sage

Overview

Plant TypePerennial
Hardiness Zone?

Cultivars

CultivarHeightSpreadBloomsSunPlanted
Phlomis russeliana36-48"18-24"Mid seasonFull SunHouse garden beds

Long-lived perennial which blooms late Spring to Autumn. Is very drought-tolerant.

Expect flowering after 1-2 years. May not come in the year when planted.

Is also very rigorous, and should be kept in check with root barriers.

Ongoing Management

Fertilization

Spring - Feed with mature compost after cutting back.

Pruning / Cutting Back

Spring - Cut back to just above the ground.

Divide after 10-15 years.
