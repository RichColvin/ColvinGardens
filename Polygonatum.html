Variegated Solomon's Seal
Polygonatum odoratum ‘Variegatum’ 		24-36" 9-12" May - June Part shade to shade House garden beds

Ongoing Management

Pruning / Cutting Back

Deadheading is not necessary.

Cut back to the base in the fall.

Propagation

 

Propagate by division in Spring, taking care not to damage young, brittle shoots.
