Insect & Disease Control

 

April 15 - Spray for fungus and insects; repeat every 7-10 days until Columbus Day

May 30

  • Spray for fungus; repeat every 7-10 days
  • Spray for insects as needed

July 04

  • Spray for fungus; repeat every 7-10 days
  • Spray for insects as needed

Labour Day / September 01

  • Spray for fungus; repeat every 7-10 days
  • Spray for insects as needed

Columbus Day / October 15 - Discontinue spraying

Fertilization

 

April 15 - Apply fertilization per instructions

May 30 - Apply fertilization per instructions

July 04 - Apply fertilization per instructions

Watering

 

May 30 - Begin watering weekly at rate of 1” / week

Columbus Day / October 15 - Discontinue watering

Pruning / Cutting Back

 

April 15 - Prune to green wood

July 04 - Remove spindly growth, especially from the centre of the plant

Columbus Day / October 15 - Stop deadheading roses to prepare them for the season

Thanksgiving Day / November 30 - Prune plants down to 30” tall to prevent whipping in Winter wind (except for climbers and large shrubs)

Other

 

April 15 - Remove Winter cover

Thanksgiving Day / November 30 - Cover base of roses with mulch, leaves, or top soil.
